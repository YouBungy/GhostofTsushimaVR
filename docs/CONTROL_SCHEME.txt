Left stick - movement

Right stick - snap turn
Right stick down - crouch
Right grip - grab
Left grip - grab
Y - quick attack/assassinate

B - dash (IF HOLDING THROWABLE, CYCLE ITEM)
A - jump
three line button (left controller) - menu
Right trigger - Fire/shoot blowdart if held, otherwise focus
Left trigger - Fire/shoot held item / if standoff, standoff

Heal, Photomode, call horse, play flute, and recenter view
