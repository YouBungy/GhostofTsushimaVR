[GENERAL]
Hold right hand up to head and press trigger - wind sense
Hold left hand up to head and press trigger - HUD/Quest objectives

[QUEST 3]
Left stick - movement
Left stick down - run
Right stick - snap turn
Right stick down - crouch
Right grip - grab
Left grip - grab
Y - quick attack/assassinate
X - quick press: heal; hold: weapon wheel
B - dash (IF HOLDING THROWABLE, CYCLE ITEM)
A - jump
three line button (left controller) - menu
Right trigger - the game's own right trigger (interact / pick up supplies); standoff when the standoff prompt is shown; held off while the katana is out (heavy charge)
Left trigger - focus when not holding anything; slow-down time when holding the bow (archery, later)

[WHEEL OPTIONS]
Heal, Photomode, call horse, play flute, and recenter view
Use right stick to select wheel option, release to confirm choice and close wheel, otherwise release left trigger to close wheel
